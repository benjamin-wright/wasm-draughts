A quick reminder of the rules
  • Charcoal moves first. Take turns on this device.
  • Men move one square diagonally forward. Select a piece, then a highlighted square.
  • Captures are compulsory. Jump over an opponent and continue jumping with that piece while possible.
  • Reach the far edge to become a king. Crowning ends your turn; kings can move and jump in either direction.
  • Win by taking every opposing piece or leaving your opponent with no legal move.
  • No automatic draws. Agree a draw between yourselves and start a new game.
